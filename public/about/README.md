# Personal photographs

Add your photographs here and set their `src` in `src/personalStory.js`.

Suggested paths:

- `/about/portrait.jpg`: a portrait or candid of Debapallab.
- `/about/dibrugarh.jpg`: home, school, or a place in Dibrugarh.
- `/about/school.jpg`: school-era design or Snapchat days.
- `/about/nagpur.jpg`: campus, friends, or everyday life at IIIT Nagpur.
- `/about/manthan.jpg`: the team, the win, or meeting the Chief Minister.
- `/about/music.jpg`: singing, playing guitar, or a performance.

Use portrait photographs where possible. The story frame is 4:5 on desktop,
and adapts on phones. Update the corresponding alt text to describe the real
photograph. Until a path is supplied, its reserved space remains intentional.
